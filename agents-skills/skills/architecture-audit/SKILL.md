# architecture-audit

## What this skill owns

**Read `references/roast.md` before the first spawn of the roast loop, rather than reconstructing the round from memory.** The loop's merge, declines, re-spawn and exit condition live there and nowhere else.

**Hand `references/semantic-audit.md` to the semantic subagent as its prompt, whole and unchanged.** The pass owns the drift categories, the WARN and UNCLAIMED row shapes and the closing counts, and the audit report template `architect` carries receives those rows as written.

**Judge in either pass against the format the `architecture-md` skill owns, never against a reconstruction of it from memory.** The reviewer brief and the semantic prompt each carry their own way of reaching it, and neither needs it restated here.

`architect` routes the four procedures - its procedure 2 roasts the contract, its procedure 4 validates the code - and loads this skill for the two passes that read and judge: the roast loop over the contract file, and the semantic pass over the codebase.

## How another skill reaches these passes

**Reference this skill by name - `architecture-audit` - from any doc or brief that needs either pass, never a pasted copy of the loop or the prompt.** A pasted body drifts from the home the passes are edited in, and a spawner holding it runs a round nobody chose.
